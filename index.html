MailHub

MailHub is a private mail client that Steve Keller runs for his own mailboxes. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

It connects to Steve's own Gmail account through Google's Gmail API to read, label, file and send his mail, and keeps a copy of that mail on his own servers so he can search it.
